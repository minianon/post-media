#4 · 6:00 PM · Thread (4 tweets) · Thread · System Design · circuit breaker
🖼 https://raw.githubusercontent.com/minianon/post-media/main/2026/11-20/x-1800-1.png

1/4 Your service calls a payment API. The API starts timing out.

Every request now waits 30 seconds, retries, and piles on more load. Your own app goes down with it.

A circuit breaker stops that spiral.

2/4 It has three states.

Closed: requests flow normally.
Open: after too many failures, calls fail fast. No waiting, no extra load on the struggling service.

3/4 Half-open: after a timeout, let a few trial requests through.

Trials succeed: back to closed.
Trials fail: back to open and wait again.

4/4 The point is to fail fast and give the dependency room to recover. Pair it with a sensible fallback message.
