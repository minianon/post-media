How do I ask for a referral as a fresher without sounding desperate?

This is a common question from freshers. Here is how I think about it.

A referral is not a favour for your name. It is a way for someone to pass along a candidate who is clearly a fit. Your job is to make that easy and safe for them.

What works:
- Read the job first. Ask only for roles that match your skills.
- Send a short note: who you are, which role, and why you fit in two lines.
- Attach your resume and one link that proves your work, such as GitHub or a project.
- Make it easy to forward: the job link and role ID in the same message.
- Follow up once after a week, then move on politely.

What does not work:
- A long life story in the first message.
- Asking strangers without any context about the role.
- Copy-pasted notes sent to ten people at once.

Even if the answer is no, thank them. People remember how you handle that.

Have you asked for a referral before? What got you a reply?

[1] (177 chars)
1/4 A payment API starts timing out. Every request waits 30 seconds, threads pile up, and now your whole app is down, not just one feature.

A circuit breaker stops that spiral.

[2] (198 chars)
2/4 It has 3 states.

Closed: requests flow normally.
Open: after too many failures, calls fail fast without touching the broken service.
Half-open: after a timeout, a few trial requests go through.

[3] (188 chars)
3/4 Trials succeed? Back to closed. Trials fail? Back to open and wait again.

The point: a failing dependency gets breathing room and your users get a quick fallback instead of a spinner.
