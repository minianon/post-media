Nobody tells freshers this about off-campus hiring:

Most rejections aren't about your skill.
They're about your signal.

A recruiter spends under a minute on your profile. What they look for:

→ Proof you finish things (a live project, not 12 half-built repos)
→ A resume that says what you did and what changed
→ A message that's specific to the role, not copy-pasted to 200 people
→ Calm, clear answers when they push on your own projects

What doesn't help as much as you think:

→ Collecting certificates
→ Applying to 100 roles in one night
→ Adding every tech keyword you've ever touched

What helps most is making your work easy to verify. A link they can click beats a line they have to trust.

Pick 5 roles you'd genuinely want. Tailor each application. Follow up once, politely.

Fewer applications, better signal.

What's one thing on your profile a recruiter can verify in 30 seconds?
