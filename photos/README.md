# Your photos

Drop your own photos here (JPG/PNG, ideally under 5 MB each) and describe each one in `captions.json`.
LinkedIn posts about your journey, mentoring, events or work life will use a matching photo.

Only photos listed in `captions.json` are ever used. Each photo is used at most once every 30 days.
Photos are never edited, filtered or generated — they're posted exactly as you upload them.

```json
{
  "photos": [
    { "file": "microsoft-office.jpg", "caption": "At the Microsoft Bengaluru office during my internship", "tags": ["microsoft", "internship", "career"] },
    { "file": "times-square.jpg", "caption": "Topmate feature on Times Square", "tags": ["mentoring", "milestone"] }
  ]
}
```
